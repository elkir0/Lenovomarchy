# Mainline Linux and Mesa status of the MediaTek MT6785 (Helio G90 / G90T / G95) for the Lenovo Yoga Tab 11 (YT-J706F)

Research date: 2026-11-15. The mainline reference is Linus Torvalds' tree at commit `23c62304dde2` (2026-11-14, **v7.4-rc2**), read with a sparse clone. The search covered `Documentation/devicetree/bindings`, `arch/arm64/boot/dts/mediatek`, `include/dt-bindings`, `sound/soc/{mediatek,codecs}`, `MAINTAINERS` and these driver directories: clk, pinctrl, gpu/drm/{mediatek,panfrost,panthor}, soc/mediatek, pmdomain/mediatek, iommu, watchdog, regulator, mfd, rtc, thermal/mediatek, cpufreq, cpuidle, phy/mediatek, usb/mtu3, mmc/host, ufs, i2c, spi, pwm, input/keyboard, iio/adc, remoteproc, mailbox, nvmem, bluetooth, net/wireless/mediatek, media/platform/mediatek and power/supply. lore.kernel.org could not be queried: it serves a bot-check page to scripts. patchwork.kernel.org (its API) was used instead.

## 1. Is there an upstream mt6785.dtsi, an upstream MT6785 board, or any MT6785 series on the lists?

### Takeaway
No. As of v7.4-rc2 (2026-11-14), mainline Linux has **zero** references to MT6785: no dtsi, no board, no binding, no driver data. No patch with "MT6785" in its subject has been posted to any kernel.org patchwork project. All MT6785 mainline work is out of tree, in Dinolek's `mt6785-mainline` kernel, which is rebased on Linux 7.1 and packaged by postmarketOS (now renamed Nura).

### Inferences
- Nothing about MT6785 is "in review" upstream: patchwork shows no posted series. The Dinolek tree is upstream-shaped but unsent. Upstreaming would mean posting roughly 15-20 small series (bindings, clk, pinctrl, pmdomain, smi/iommu, mmsys/drm, panfrost, ufs, usb, thermal, cpufreq, dts). The code is already written, so the remaining work is review iterations.
- For the YT-J706F, the realistic base is the Dinolek 7.1 tree with a new board dts. Waiting for mainline is not realistic.

### Gaps
- lore.kernel.org full-text search (which also covers message bodies and replies) could not be run because of its bot-check page. A series whose subject avoided "MT6785" would be missed by the subject-based patchwork search. This is unlikely, but not excluded.
- linux-next was not checked separately. Since nothing was posted, nothing can be queued there.
- The GitLab group `gitlab.postmarketos.org/mt6785-mainline` could not be listed (its API did not return JSON to scripts). It may hold branches newer than the GitHub mirror.

## 2. Block-by-block status of MT6785 support (mainline vs out of tree)

### Takeaway
**Merged upstream for MT6785 itself: nothing.** The PMIC side is the exception: MT6359 (main PMIC) and MT6360 (charger/TCPC/LDO, used on begonia) are mainline, since Chromebooks (MT8192/MT8195/MT8188) use the same chips. The Dinolek tree covers every SoC block needed for a usable desktop: clocks, pinctrl, power domains, SMI/IOMMU, display, Panfrost GPU, UFS/MMC, USB, I2C/SPI/PWM, thermal, cpufreq, watchdog. Most of these reuse existing mainline IP through fallback compatibles. There is **nothing anywhere (upstream or credible mainline out-of-tree)** for audio AFE, Wi-Fi/BT CONSYS, camera ISP, video codecs or the modem.

### Inferences
- A rough "to write" estimate for the YT-J706F, on top of the Dinolek tree: (a) a new board dts (panel, touch, backlight, charger, speakers); (b) finishing the T-PHY "wip" patch; (c) everything listed as "nothing / none": audio AFE (port downstream `sound/soc/mediatek/mt6785`, probably modelled on the mt8183/mt8192 AFE drivers; the MT6359 codec side already exists), Wi-Fi/BT (WMT + gen4m, a large vendor stack never ported to mainline), camera, video codec, suspend. Wi-Fi is the biggest functional risk for a tablet without a modem. Plan for a USB Wi-Fi/Ethernet dongle or a multi-month port.
- The fact that display, GPU and cpufreq needed only 1-10 driver lines each is strong evidence that MT6785 IP is near-identical to MT8183/MT8192/MT8186 for those blocks. That lowers the risk on the parts that matter most for Hyprland: DRM/KMS + Panfrost.
- Clocks, pinctrl and power domains are the large SoC-specific blocks (about 7,000 lines combined). They are already written OOT, but no upstream reviewer has seen them.

### Gaps
- Whether suspend-to-RAM works, and its CPU-idle state, is not documented anywhere (the SoC page's `status_suspend` is empty, and the begonia page has no suspend field).
- The YT-J706F's own peripherals (DSI panel and possible bridge, touchscreen controller, charger IC, speaker amplifiers, eMMC vs UFS storage) were not verified: no Lenovo kernel source or DT dump was read. One Android/TWRP device tree exists, [jeremywildsmith/android_device_lenovo_yoga11](https://awesome.ecosyste.ms/projects/github.com%2Fjeremywildsmith%2Fandroid_device_lenovo_yoga11) (last push Dec 2024, generated by a TWRP tree generator), but it was not inspected.
- The exact mainline release in which `mt6779-pwrap`, `mt6779-m4u`, `mt6779-mmc` and `mt6779-gce` landed was not verified. They exist in v7.4-rc2; their series were posted between 2019-10 and 2020-06 per patchwork.

## 3. Closely related SoCs as templates, and how much IP they share with MT6785

### Takeaway
The best code templates are **MT8183** (display, DSI/MIPI-TX, MMC, UFS PHY, PWM, AFE structure, Panfrost glue), **MT8192/MT8186** (OVL, CCI, I2C, LVTS, MT6359 PMIC integration, regulator coupler), and **MT6779** (same-generation phone SoC: GCE, SMI-common, pinctrl/clock layout; but no GPU or DRM upstream, and its GPU is PowerVR). MT6789/MT6768/MT6765 are weaker templates: MT6789 is only in a close-to-mainline fork, MT6768 mainline work stalled in 2023, and MT6765 has only clocks/pinctrl/SPI/timer upstream.

### Cited Findings
- **MT6779 (Helio P90)** mainline content: clocks (v5.4), pinctrl (v5.9), dtsi + EVB (v5.11), keypad driver (v5.18), plus `mt6779-pwrap`, `mt6779-m4u`, `mt6779-mmc`, `mt6779-gce`, `mt6779-mmsys`, `mt6779-devapc` and SPI/UART bindings. There are **no** MT6779 power domains, display/DRM, GPU, AFE or thermal entries — [mt6779.dtsi @ v7.4-rc2](https://github.com/torvalds/linux/blob/23c62304dde29abe92013d85910d03a77dada35d/arch/arm64/boot/dts/mediatek/mt6779.dtsi), [keypad commit f28af984e771](https://github.com/torvalds/linux/commit/f28af984e771)
- MT6779 was upstreamed by MediaTek engineers in 2019-2020: Mars Cheng / Hanks Chen (basic SoC, v1 2019-08-05 to v5 2020-03-25), Argus Lin (pwrap, 2019-10/2020-02), Chao Hao (IOMMU, 2019-11 to 2020-05), Ming-Fan Chen (SMI), chun-hung.wu (MMC v6, 2020-06) — [patchwork linux-mediatek q=mt6779](https://patchwork.kernel.org/project/linux-mediatek/list/?q=mt6779&archive=both&state=*)
- The Helio P90 (MT6779) GPU is a **PowerVR GM 9446**, not a Mali — [MediaTek Helio P90 product page](https://www.mediatek.com/products/smartphones/mediatek-helio-p90); [Notebookcheck PowerVR GM9446](https://www.notebookcheck.net/PowerVR-GM9446-GPU-Benchmarks-and-Specs.455373.0.html)
- MT6785 per the postmarketOS/Nura SoC page: 2x Cortex-A76 at 2.05 GHz + 6x Cortex-A55 at 2.0 GHz, "ARM Mali-G76 3EEMC4 @ (800/900) MHz", 12 nm, codenames mt6785v/cc/cd. G90T and G95 differ only in GPU clock (800 vs 900 MHz) — [wiki: MediaTek Helio G90T/G95 (MT6785)](https://wiki.nura.eco/wiki/MediaTek_Helio_G90T/G95_(MT6785))
- IP reuse in the out-of-tree MT6785 dtsi, by DT fallback compatible: `mt6779-gce`, `mt6779-smi-common`, `mt8183-mmc`, `mt8183-dsi`, `mt8183-mipi-tx`, `mt8183-disp-rdma/ccorr/aal/gamma/dither`, `mt8183-disp-pwm`, `mt8183-pwm`, `mt8183-ufsphy`, `mt8192-disp-ovl`, `mt8192-disp-ovl-2l`, `mt8192-disp-postmask`, `mt8173-disp-color`, `mt8186-i2c`, `mt8186-cci`, `mt6765-spi`, `mt6765-timer`, `mt6577-uart`, `mt6577-sysirq`, `generic-tphy-v2` — [Dinolek mt6785.dtsi](https://github.com/Dinolek/mt6785-mainline/blob/7.1/arch/arm64/boot/dts/mediatek/mt6785.dtsi)
- **MT8183** (Chromebooks, Mali-G72 MP3): Panfrost compatible since v5.14 — [Panfrost mt8183 commit 1275e4175368](https://github.com/torvalds/linux/commit/1275e4175368); [Phoronix, "Panfrost ... Adding Mediatek MT8183 Support With Linux 5.14"](https://www.phoronix.com/news/Mediatek-MT8183-Panfrost)
- **MT8186** (Mali-G52) and **MT8192** (Mali-G57) Panfrost compatibles since v6.4; MT8188 since v6.11; MT8370 since v6.17. The MediaTek Panfrost platform data gained clock/regulator-off-in-suspend features (`GPU_PM_CLK_DIS`, `GPU_PM_VREG_OFF`) in 2023-11 — [mt8186 901cdf66e86a](https://github.com/torvalds/linux/commit/901cdf66e86a), [mt8192 4c6811800cb4](https://github.com/torvalds/linux/commit/4c6811800cb4), [mt8188 72533b678405](https://github.com/torvalds/linux/commit/72533b678405), [mt8370 81645377c231](https://github.com/torvalds/linux/commit/81645377c231), [regulators on/off 540527b1385f](https://github.com/torvalds/linux/commit/540527b1385f)
- MT6359 (the PMIC on begonia) is shared with mainline Chromebook platforms: MT8192 and MT8195 machine drivers `mt8192-mt6359-rt1015-rt5682`, `mt8195-mt6359`, `mt8188-mt6359` — [sound/soc/mediatek @ v7.4-rc2](https://github.com/torvalds/linux/tree/23c62304dde29abe92013d85910d03a77dada35d/sound/soc/mediatek)
- **MT6789 (Helio G99)**: clock-controller, pwrap and SPI patches by Arseniy Velikanov were posted on 2025-07-15 (v1) and 2025-07-18 (v2 clocks). They are **not merged** (no `mt6789` string in v7.4-rc2) — [patchwork linux-mediatek q=mt6789](https://patchwork.kernel.org/project/linux-mediatek/list/?q=mt6789&archive=both&state=*). The Nura/pmOS wiki lists MT6789 as `mainline = partial` (GPU Y, display P, USB Y, storage Y, thermal Y, audio/Wi-Fi/BT/camera/suspend N) with a "close-to-mainline fork" at `github.com/linux-mediatek/linux/tree/mt6789-next` — [wiki: MediaTek Helio G99 (MT6789/MT8781)](https://wiki.nura.eco/wiki/MediaTek_Helio_G99_(MT6789/MT8781))
- **MT6768/MT6769Z (Helio G85)**: a mainline fork at `gitlab.com/mtk-mainline/mt6768/linux` booted the Volla Phone 22 on v6.1-rc5 with Sxmo. The fork has had no visible activity since January 2023 (per search summary) — [Volla Phone 22 wiki](https://wiki.postmarketos.org/wiki/Volla_Phone_22_(volla-mimameid)), [pmaports MR 3652](https://gitlab.com/postmarketOS/pmaports/-/merge_requests/3652). Mainline has no `mt6768`/`mt6769` string (v7.4-rc2 grep).
- **MT6765 (Helio P35)**: mainline has clocks (v5.8), pinctrl, SPI and timer, but no `mt6765.dtsi` in `dts/mediatek`. A dtsi was posted (v8 by Mars Cheng) and an RFC for a Bigme tablet was posted on the postmarketOS upstreaming list — [clk-mt6765 commit 1aca9939bf72](https://github.com/torvalds/linux/commit/1aca9939bf72), [spinics v8 dts](https://www.spinics.net/lists/kernel/msg3415828.html), [sr.ht RFC](https://lists.sr.ht/~postmarketos/upstreaming/patches/42410)
- Recent upstream precedent for phone SoCs: **MT6893 (Dimensity 1200)** pinctrl and power domains, by AngeloGioacchino Del Regno (Collabora), landed in v6.16, together with mt6893 IOMMU, T-PHY, UFS, SPI and DVFSRC entries. **MT6735** power domains (Yassine Oudjana, community) landed in v6.13, along with clocks, pinctrl and watchdog. Mainline also contains mt6858/mt6878/mt6991 pinctrl/UFS/PWM bits — [mt6893 pinctrl 8004507179c8](https://github.com/torvalds/linux/commit/8004507179c8), [mt6893 pmdomain 5342f018e9e2](https://github.com/torvalds/linux/commit/5342f018e9e2), [mt6735 pmdomain c2114a0d1763](https://github.com/torvalds/linux/commit/c2114a0d1763)
- Another older MediaTek phone SoC fully brought up by a community member: AngeloGioacchino's 2022 "MediaTek Helio X10 MT6795" series (pinctrl, power domains, SMI, IOMMU, clocks, pwrap, MT6331/6332 PMIC), now with an upstream Sony Xperia M5 board — [patchwork linux-mediatek q=Helio](https://patchwork.kernel.org/project/linux-mediatek/list/?q=Helio&archive=both&state=*), [mt6795-sony-xperia-m5.dts](https://github.com/torvalds/linux/blob/23c62304dde29abe92013d85910d03a77dada35d/arch/arm64/boot/dts/mediatek/mt6795-sony-xperia-m5.dts)

### Inferences
- For the work still missing (audio AFE, upstream submission), MT8183 and MT8192 are the practical references: same display/DSI IP, MT6359 PMIC in the MT8192 case, and both have complete AFE + machine drivers upstream. MT6779 helps mostly for clock/pinctrl/infra register layouts (same generation), but it has no DRM, GPU, AFE or power-domain code upstream.
- The MT6795, MT6735 and MT6893 precedents show maintainers accept phone-SoC enablement series in 2022-2025, so MT6785 upstreaming is procedurally realistic. The "ARM/Mediatek SoC support" maintainers in v7.4-rc2 are Matthias Brugger and AngeloGioacchino Del Regno (Collabora), per [MAINTAINERS](https://github.com/torvalds/linux/blob/23c62304dde29abe92013d85910d03a77dada35d/MAINTAINERS).
- MT6789 and MT6768 should not be used as templates: they are not upstream, and the MT6768 effort is stale. Their GPUs are also, from general knowledge not verified in this session, a Mali-G57 MC2 (Valhall) and a Mali-G52 MC2, not the G76.

### Gaps
- No public MediaTek document maps MT6785 register blocks against MT6779/MT8183. The IP-sharing evidence above is inferred from the fallback compatibles that the out-of-tree tree uses successfully; it is not a vendor statement.
- The MT6789 GPU model (G57 MC2) was not independently verified in this session.

## 4. Mesa / Panfrost on Mali-G76 (Bifrost v7): GL level, PanVK, known issues, desktop reports

### Takeaway
Mali-G76 is a supported **Bifrost v7** GPU in Panfrost (Gallium): OpenGL ES 3.1 and desktop OpenGL 3.1. The same v7 architecture is Khronos-conformant for GLES 3.1 on Mali-G52 (2021); G76 itself has no conformance submission. PanVK lists Vulkan 1.3 for v6/v7, but in Mesa main (27.0.0-devel, Nov 2026) it still **refuses to load on v7** unless `PAN_I_WANT_A_BROKEN_VULKAN_DRIVER=1` is set. No Hyprland-specific report on G76 or any Bifrost GPU was found. Weston + Xwayland + 3D are reported working on the MT6785 begonia phone.

### Cited Findings
- The Mesa Panfrost docs table lists the row "G31, G51, G52, G76 | Bifrost (v7) | 3.1 [\*] | 3.1 | 1.3 | 3.0" (OpenGL ES | OpenGL | Vulkan | OpenCL). The footnoted conformance submissions are Mali-G52 (GLES 3.1), Mali-G57 (GLES 3.1), Mali-G610 (GLES 3.1 and Vulkan 1.4), and G310/G610 (OpenCL 3.0). The docs also say: "On GPUs where PanVK support is experimental, the driver refuses to load by default. Setting PAN_I_WANT_A_BROKEN_VULKAN_DRIVER=1 enables it." (fetched 2026-11-15) — [docs.mesa3d.org Panfrost](https://docs.mesa3d.org/drivers/panfrost.html)
- In Mesa `main` (VERSION `27.0.0-devel`, last commit 2026-11-15), `panvk_physical_device.c` gates `case 6: case 7: case 11: case 14:` behind `PAN_I_WANT_A_BROKEN_VULKAN_DRIVER` with the message "panvk is not well-tested on v%d". Only v10, v12 and v13 load by default. v9 (G57) is not supported by PanVK at all (default branch: "not supported") — [panvk_physical_device.c (main)](https://gitlab.freedesktop.org/mesa/mesa/-/blob/main/src/panfrost/vulkan/panvk_physical_device.c), [Mesa VERSION](https://gitlab.freedesktop.org/mesa/mesa/-/blob/main/VERSION)

### Inferences
- Hyprland already runs on the owner's Panfrost Midgard (T760, GLES 3.1) stack, and Bifrost v7 exposes the same GLES 3.1 / GL 3.1 level with a conformant sibling (G52). So the GL feature level should not block Hyprland on the G76. The risk is G76-specific bugs: the G76 has few Panfrost users (no mainline DT user at all, almost no issue reports), so the YT-J706F would itself be the test bed.
- Vulkan on v7 is "use at your own risk". Hyprland's GLES renderer does not need it, but apps that require Vulkan would need the env var and might break.
- Panfrost (JM, not Panthor) is the right kernel driver: G76 is job-manager Bifrost, and Panthor only binds CSF GPUs (`rockchip,rk3588-mali`, `mediatek,mt8196-mali`, `arm,mali-valhall-csf`) per [panthor_drv.c](https://github.com/torvalds/linux/blob/23c62304dde29abe92013d85910d03a77dada35d/drivers/gpu/drm/panthor/panthor_drv.c).

### Gaps
- No real-world report of Hyprland, sway/wlroots or GNOME on a Mali-G76, or of Hyprland on any Bifrost GPU, was found. Only Weston on begonia (MT6785) and generic desktop use on MT8183 Chromebooks.
- Hyprland 0.57's exact minimum GLES version was not checked.
- G76 performance numbers under Panfrost (for example glmark2 on begonia) were not found.

## 5. Community mainline efforts for MT6785 devices, and how far they got

### Takeaway
One serious effort exists: **Dinolek's mt6785-mainline** (pmOS/Nura "testing" device: Xiaomi Redmi Note 8 Pro "begonia", maintainer Dinolek, contributors Michaelskyf and kittyle). It boots to a graphical session with working display, touch, 3D (Panfrost), USB networking/OTG, storage and SD card, haptics, NFC and sensors. Wi-Fi, Bluetooth, audio, battery, camera and modem do not work. The pmOS package is 6.16.4 (Sept 2025). The GitHub tree was rebased to 7.1 in June 2026. No YT-J706F or other Lenovo MT6785 mainline work was found.

- The 6.16.4 package (Sept 2025) vs the 7.1 branch (June 2026) suggests the effort is maintained at a slow cadence by one developer.

### Gaps
- No public status of suspend, cpuidle, thermal throttling or battery life for begonia on mainline.
- Not verified: whether the YT-J706F uses the same PMIC pair (MT6359 + MT6360) as begonia, its panel interface, or whether its storage is eMMC or UFS. No Lenovo GPL kernel tarball was inspected.
- The GitLab group `gitlab.postmarketos.org/mt6785-mainline` and the pmaports MR history for `linux-postmarketos-mediatek-mt6785` could not be read (bot protection / server errors). There may be newer packaged versions or an issue tracker with per-block status.
